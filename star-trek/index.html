Brad Seraphin is a Product Marketing Manager reporting for duty to the Star Trek Fleet Command team. A seasoned product marketing veteran, having recently returned from a GTM and Growth tour of duty with a small a16z-backed gaming studio, Proof of Play, and having launched Shiba Story Go! on iOS and Android, is ready to boldly go into product marketing with one of mobile gaming's deepest, most enduring, and most community-driven live-service experiences in the Galaxy.

This role sits at the intersection of Product, Live Operations, Marketing, Community, Campaigns, and Player Experience — familiar space for Brad to navigate. At HP, he held the captain's chair for exactly this kind of multi-department mission, steering GTM for the HP Smart app across a stakeholder crew numbering in the hundreds, while the app warped from 6 million to 66 million monthly active users — a fleet size not unlike the one Star Trek Fleet Command commands today.

This is where the mission profile matches Brad's actual service record. Positioning, go-to-market ownership, messaging frameworks, launch materials, creative direction, cross-functional coordination. It's the job he's done at every post. At Proof of Play, recruited as the studio's sole marketing hire, he owned it end to end for Shiba Story Go!, pivoting from underperforming "fake gameplay" ads to cinematic trailers built in hours instead of days, and managing the UA and creative partners behind a TikTok run from zero to 291,000 followers in seven weeks.

Star Trek Fleet Command is one of the most sophisticated strategy games in mobile. Full disclosure: Fleet Command itself is downloading on Brad's phone right now, for the first time. He's the new recruit here, and he knows it. But he's spent two decades training for exactly this kind of mission: translating complex systems into clear player value, keeping cross-functional crews moving together, turning ambitious ideas into things people actually want. Star Trek has been part of his life since long before this download bar finished. The game is new. The mission is not.
