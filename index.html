About

Bradley builds marketing infrastructure from zero, then runs it himself — the growth systems, the AI agents, the analytics programs, and the campaigns other marketers hand off to agencies.

His background spans the earliest days of online video (Veoh, racing YouTube), a Silicon Beach venture builder (Curious Minds, three real exits), enterprise platforms at HP and Shutterstock, and Proof of Play, where he was the company's only marketing hire — vibe-coding an AI marketing engine from a $0 budget. He builds AI-native marketing systems, not just AI-assisted workflows, and uses that infrastructure to drive measurable growth across SEO, paid, and organic channels.

In between, he founded Gift-Hippo.com and sold seven figures in promotional gifts — running the books and operations himself, not just the marketing — picked up a PMP certification from Google, and is a University of Oklahoma journalism grad who never stopped being a storyteller, just pointed it at growth curves instead of headlines.
