A $1M+ side business built on a warranty most people don't think to ask about

Founder, Gift Hippo
Founder-owned · Ongoing

Gift Hippo is an authorized distributor of custom print and laser-etched promotional gifts from major brands. The distinction that matters: because Bradley operates as an authorized distributor rather than a generic customizer, the manufacturer's warranty carries forward even after the gift is personalized — something most promotional-product buyers don't realize is even a question until it isn't true.

The business

Real product, real supplier, real revenue

Bradley partners with Hirsch Gifts as his supplier, sourcing from major brands and handling the full order flow himself: quote, mock-up, invoicing, and shipping, direct to the client deadline. Gift Hippo has sold over $1M in promotional gifts since opening — built and run as a solo, founder-owned operation alongside his marketing career, not a side hustle that stayed small.
